Delete your SnapCal account

SnapCal (com.snapcal.snapcal) · Najim Bacha · Updated 12 September 2026

You can delete your SnapCal account and its data yourself, in the app. If you cannot open the app, email us and we will do it for you.

In the app

  1. Open SnapCal.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Account.
  4. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Deletion runs immediately and cannot be undone.

By email

Email najimbacha1@gmail.com from the email address on the account, asking us to delete it. We will confirm, delete the account and reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your meals, scans and food photos
  • Water, weigh-ins, body-fat entries and progress photos
  • Your profile, goals, plans and coach conversation
  • Your settings and notification token
  • Your sign-in itself, so the account no longer exists

Everything above is removed from our servers and from the phone you delete it on.

What is not deleted

  • Your Google Play subscription. Deleting the account does not cancel it. Cancel it in Google Play, or you will keep being charged.
  • Security and audit records — for example a record that an administrator changed an account's access — which we keep for up to 12 months.
  • Crash reports, which are not linked to your name and are removed by Firebase Crashlytics on its own schedule, currently after 90 days.
  • Payment records Google Play must keep for tax and accounting.
Signing out is not deletion. Signing out leaves your account and its backup intact so you can come back; deletion removes them.
